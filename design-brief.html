★ Website Design & Vision Brief

STRIVE

Build your future.

A brief for our designer and developer: what we're building, how it should feel, and how it scales. Launching with Psychology & Research.

01 / VISION
The brand

A career platform, not a career service.

Strive helps people make better career decisions, build meaningful careers, develop professional skills and navigate transitions. Built for all careers, launching with Psychology & Research, growing into profession-specific ecosystems.

Intelligent

Premium

Warm

Editorial

Aspirational

Not a counselling site, academic institution, corporate HR page or generic coaching site. A visitor from LinkedIn should think: "This is a serious, modern career platform."

04 / THE STRIVE MODEL
One career. Many stages.

Five layers, one continuum.

The Decision Layer16–18

Informed career decisions before choosing a direction.

The Build Layer18–25

Turning direction into education, experience, skills and opportunity.

The Skill Layer25–35

Professional skills, visibility and assets to grow.

The SpecificsPROFESSION-SPECIFIC

Deep dives via workshops, webinars, resources and guidance.

The Transition Layer40+

Career change, reinvention and what's next.

Design note: a connected line/timeline, never five separate boxes. Permanent part of brand architecture.

05 / KEY PAGES
Supporting pages

Each page has one clear job.

Strive Specifics

"Go deeper into your profession." Now: Psychology Careers, Research Careers, Workshops & Webinars. Then: Coming Next. Built so professions can be added without a redesign.

Journal

"Think better about your career." Editorial publication feel, not a blog: strong headlines, clean cards, whitespace. Topics: Career Psychology, Decisions, Growth, Psychology & Research, Work & Careers. CTA: Read on Substack.

About / Founder

"Built from psychology. Designed for careers." Inaayat Khanna, psychologist. Large monochrome portrait; personal and credible, not a résumé. CTA: LinkedIn.

Work With Strive

"Your next step starts here." Choose · Build · Grow · Specialise · Transition. CTAs: Explore Current Offers, Connect With Strive.
